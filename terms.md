# Personal Health Monitor — Terms of Use

Last updated: 15 October 2026

## Intended use

Personal Health Monitor is a personal prototype for its owner to reflect on wellness goals and health trends in Chinese-language conversations. It is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oura or OpenAI. The current project is not offered as a public health service.

## Available and planned features

The project is preparing a private conversation workflow for organizing user-stated goals and subjective check-ins. Live wearable-data access, automated reports and continuous monitoring are not currently available. Planned connected features must be verified and appropriately authorized before being described as active.

## Limitations

Reports and conversational suggestions may contain errors and may rely on incomplete or outdated information. They provide general wellness information, not medical diagnosis, treatment, medication decisions or emergency monitoring. No particular health outcome or continuous availability is promised.

## Access and third-party services

The owner controls local credentials, ChatGPT permissions and any future provider authorization. Private records and secrets must not be shared in this public repository. Use of GitHub, ChatGPT and any future connected provider remains subject to the relevant provider's terms.

## Stopping use

The owner can remove the private plugin, stop local processes, delete local credentials and revoke any future connected-provider access. Those actions do not automatically erase information already held by third-party services; their account and deletion controls govern that information.

Contact the project owner through the GitHub account that owns this repository. Do not include personal health records in public issues.
